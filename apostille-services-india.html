What is Apostille?

An Apostille is an official certification issued by the Ministry of External Affairs (MEA), Government of India. It validates the authenticity of signatures, seals and stamps appearing on public documents. Apostille certification enables documents issued in India to be recognized in countries that are members of the Hague Apostille Convention.

Apostille Process in India

Step 1 - Document Verification

Documents are verified by the appropriate issuing authority before submission for apostille certification.

Step 2 - State Authentication

Certain documents may require prior authentication from state-level departments before apostille processing.

Step 3 - MEA Apostille

The Ministry of External Affairs issues the Apostille certificate, making the document valid for use in Hague Convention countries.

Countries Accepting Apostille Documents

Apostille-certified documents are accepted in many countries including Germany, France, Italy, Spain, Netherlands, Portugal, Belgium, Austria, Poland and several other Hague Convention member nations.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Apostille?

Apostille is an international certification that confirms the authenticity of a public document.

Who issues Apostille in India?

The Ministry of External Affairs (MEA), Government of India, issues Apostille certification.

Which documents can be Apostilled?

Educational, personal and commercial documents can be Apostilled based on eligibility and verification requirements.

Is Apostille different from Embassy Attestation?

Yes. Apostille is accepted by Hague Convention countries, whereas embassy attestation is generally required for non-Hague Convention countries.
