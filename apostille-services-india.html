What is Apostille?

An Apostille is an official certification issued by the Ministry of External Affairs (MEA), Government of India. It validates the authenticity of signatures, seals and stamps appearing on public documents. Apostille certification enables documents issued in India to be recognized in countries that are members of the Hague Apostille Convention.

Apostille Process in India

Step 1 - Document Verification

Documents are verified by the appropriate issuing authority before submission for apostille certification.

Step 2 - State Authentication

Certain documents may require prior authentication from state-level departments before apostille processing.

Step 3 - MEA Apostille

The Ministry of External Affairs issues the Apostille certificate, making the document valid for use in Hague Convention countries.

Countries Accepting Apostille Documents

Apostille-certified documents are accepted in many countries including Germany, France, Italy, Spain, Netherlands, Portugal, Belgium, Austria, Poland and several other Hague Convention member nations.

Types of Documents Eligible for Apostille in India

Apostille certification can be obtained for a wide range of educational, personal and commercial documents intended for use in Hague Convention member countries. The type of document determines the authentication process required before Apostille issuance.

Educational Documents

Educational documents commonly submitted for Apostille include Degree Certificates, Diploma Certificates, Mark Sheets, Transcripts, HSC Certificates, SSLC Certificates and other academic records. These documents are often required for higher education, employment and professional licensing abroad.

Personal Documents

Personal documents eligible for Apostille include Birth Certificates, Marriage Certificates, Police Clearance Certificates (PCC), Divorce Certificates and Death Certificates. These documents may be required for immigration, family sponsorship and residence applications.

Commercial Documents

Commercial documents such as Certificates of Incorporation, Board Resolutions, Power of Attorney, Export Documents and Business Agreements can also be Apostilled for international business activities.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Apostille?

Apostille is an international certification that confirms the authenticity of a public document.

Who issues Apostille in India?

The Ministry of External Affairs (MEA), Government of India, issues Apostille certification.

Which documents can be Apostilled?

Educational, personal and commercial documents can be Apostilled based on eligibility and verification requirements.

Is Apostille different from Embassy Attestation?

Yes. Apostille is accepted by Hague Convention countries, whereas embassy attestation is generally required for non-Hague Convention countries.

How long does Apostille take in India?

Processing time may vary depending on document type, issuing authority requirements and authentication procedures.

Can Degree Certificates be Apostilled?

Yes. Degree Certificates issued by recognized educational institutions can be Apostilled after the required verification process.

Can Birth Certificates be Apostilled?

Yes. Birth Certificates are among the most commonly Apostilled personal documents.

Is Apostille accepted in Germany?

Yes. Germany is one of the countries that accepts Apostille-certified documents under the Hague Convention.

Can commercial documents be Apostilled?

Yes. Various commercial and business-related documents may be eligible for Apostille certification.
